Beautiful Places in Vietnam

A simple HTML5 page about three amazing destinations in Vietnam.

Featured Destinations

Ha Long Bay

Ha Long Bay is famous for its emerald water and thousands of limestone islands and islets.

Da Nang

Da Nang is a coastal city known for its beaches, mountains, and modern city life.

Hoi An

Hoi An is a historic town known for its traditional architecture, colorful lanterns, and cultural heritage.
